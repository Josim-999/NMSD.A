## Immediate actions
1. Driver secures the vehicle, switches on hazard lights and places warning triangles.
2. Driver reports the breakdown in the driver app and calls the coordinator.
3. Coordinator requests road assistance from the contracted fleet maintenance provider and records the request number.

## Load protection
- For refrigerated loads, confirm the reefer unit is still running. If the reefer unit is down, a replacement truck must be dispatched immediately.
- If repair time is estimated above 90 minutes, dispatch the nearest available truck of the same type to transfer the load.
- Inform affected customers with a new ETA.

## Closure
Fleet team records the cause, downtime and cost against the truck in the maintenance system.
