## Limits (sample company policy)
- Maximum driving time: 10 hours per day.
- Mandatory break: 30 minutes after every 4.5 hours of driving.
- Minimum daily rest: 11 consecutive hours.

## Planning rule
A trip must not be assigned to a driver if the planned driving time of the trip is more than the driver's remaining driving time for the day. Long-haul drivers rest at the destination hub before returning.
