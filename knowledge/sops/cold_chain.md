## Temperature ranges
- Refrigerated pharmaceutical products: 2 to 8 degrees C.
- Ambient pharmaceutical products: 15 to 25 degrees C.

## Rules
- Medical and pharmaceutical shipments flagged as refrigerated may only be loaded on reefer trucks or reefer vans.
- The reefer unit must be pre-cooled for 30 minutes before loading.
- A temperature excursion of more than 15 minutes outside range must be reported to Quality before delivery.
- Maximum time outside the reefer during unloading is 10 minutes.
