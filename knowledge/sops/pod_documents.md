## Before dispatch
- Every shipment must have a manifest number, pallet count and customer reference in the TMS before the truck leaves.
- A truck must not be released by the gate if any shipment on it has no manifest number.

## At delivery
- POD must show receiver name, signature, stamp, date and time.
- Driver uploads a photo of the signed POD in the driver app within 15 minutes of delivery.
- Damaged or short deliveries are recorded on the POD with a photo.
