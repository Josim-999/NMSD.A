## Delay thresholds
- Delay of 20 to 44 minutes: Coordinator contacts the driver to confirm the cause and updates the ETA in the TMS.
- Delay of 45 minutes or more: Coordinator informs the customer with a new ETA and notifies the Transportation Supervisor.
- Delay of more than 2 hours or any risk of missing a hospital delivery window: Supervisor escalates to the Operations Manager.

## Recording
Every delay of 20 minutes or more must be closed with a reason code: DOCK_WAIT, TRAFFIC, LATE_LOADING, BREAKDOWN, ROUTE, DRIVER, CUSTOMER_CLOSED or OTHER.

## Chronic waiting customers
If a customer causes waiting longer than 30 minutes on more than 25% of visits in a month, the planner must add a waiting buffer to that customer in the routing plan and the account manager must raise it with the customer.
