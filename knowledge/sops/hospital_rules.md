## Appointments
Hospitals flagged with appointment required must have a confirmed booking slot before the truck departs. Arriving without a slot can cause refusal.

## Priority
Hospital deliveries have priority over pharmacy and clinic deliveries when a truck is delayed and the sequence must be changed. Changing the stop sequence requires coordinator approval.

## Receiving
Deliveries are received only at the designated medical stores dock, never at the main hospital entrance.
