## Subcontracted trucks
- Riyadh city trip: 650 SAR per trip.
- Riyadh to Dammam: 2,400 SAR per trip.
- Waiting time charged after 2 hours: 120 SAR per hour.

## Penalties
Late delivery penalty to hospitals: 2% of the delivery value per hour after the agreed window, capped at 10%.
