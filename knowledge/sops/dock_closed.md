## When the driver arrives and the receiving dock is closed
1. Driver must not leave the goods unattended or hand them to security staff without a receiving signature.
2. Driver calls the Transportation Coordinator immediately and shares a photo of the closed dock with a timestamp.
3. Coordinator calls the customer receiving contact listed in the customer master data. Wait a maximum of 30 minutes for the dock to open.
4. If the dock does not open within 30 minutes, coordinator agrees a new delivery slot with the customer and records the reason code DOCK_CLOSED in the TMS.
5. For refrigerated goods, check the remaining cold-chain time before deciding to wait or return (see SOP-CC-01).

## When arrival is predicted after dock closing time
- Call the customer before the truck arrives and request an extension of receiving hours.
- If no extension is possible, re-plan the stop for the next morning first slot and inform the account manager.
- Never send a truck to a closed hospital dock without written confirmation from the customer.
