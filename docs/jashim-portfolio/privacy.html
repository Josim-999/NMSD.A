Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is collected, used, shared, and protected when you visit a website or use a service operated by Jashim Uddin (“we”, “us”, or “the operator”). By using our sites or services, you agree to the practices described here.

ⓘ
Scope. This policy applies to the websites and digital services operated by Jashim Uddin, including portfolio, business, and client-facing sites that link to this page. Where a specific site or client project has its own separate policy, that policy applies to that project.

01 Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you provide. When you contact us, request a quote, fill in a form, or email us, we receive the details you choose to share — such as your name, email address, phone number, business name, and the content of your message.
  • Information collected automatically. When you visit our sites, standard technical data may be recorded, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed, referring page, and the date and time of your visit.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. Our sites and hosting provider may use cookies or local storage for essential functionality and basic analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser; some features may not work as intended if you do.

02 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide the services you request;
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve our websites and services;
  • Communicate with you about projects, quotes, updates, or support;
  • Understand how our sites are used, so we can improve them;
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

03 Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on: your consent (which you may withdraw at any time); the performance of a contract with you; our legitimate interests in operating and improving our business; and compliance with legal obligations.

04 Sharing and Disclosure

We may share limited information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate our sites and services — for example, hosting and deployment platforms (such as Vercel), email providers, and analytics tools — who are permitted to use the data only to perform services for us;
  • Authorities where disclosure is required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and property;
  • A successor entity in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

05 Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, to provide our services, to comply with legal obligations, to resolve disputes, and to enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

06 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • Request deletion of your information;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing;
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

07 Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against loss, misuse, and unauthorised access. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

08 International Transfers

Our hosting and service providers may store or process data on servers located outside your country, including outside Saudi Arabia. Where this happens, we take steps to ensure your information remains protected in line with this policy and applicable law.

09 Children’s Privacy

Our sites and services are not directed at children under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.
